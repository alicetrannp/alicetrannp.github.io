About this practice

The rare case where in-person care is genuinely convenient.

Fair Oaks sits at the crossroads of Fairfax County: the mall and its orbit of offices, Inova Fair Oaks Hospital, Fair Lakes’ corporate campuses, and neighborhoods full of families running on I-66 and Route 50 schedules. Most of this website talks about telehealth, but for Fair Oaks residents the calculus flips: the office at 3060 Williams Drive in Merrifield is roughly a ten-minute drive, which makes fully in-person psychiatric care a realistic first choice.

The people who come from the Fair Oaks area look like the area itself: healthcare workers from Inova Fair Oaks who spend their days caring for everyone else; professionals from the Fair Lakes corridor carrying quiet, high-functioning anxiety; parents juggling two careers and a school calendar; and a wide mix of immigrant families, including many Vietnamese and Korean households, for whom care in their own language changes everything.

Alice Tran is a dual board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner (PMHNP-BC, FNP-BC), trained under a psychiatrist with more than fifty years of experience. She treats adults 18 to 65 for anxiety, depression, ADHD, OCD, PTSD, bipolar disorder, insomnia, and burnout, offering psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and supportive psychotherapy in English and Tiếng Việt.

You choose the format visit by visit: in person at the office, with free parking and no hospital-campus maze, or secure telehealth from home or work. Most major insurance is accepted, including Aetna, Anthem, Cigna, Medicare, and Medicaid, and new patients are usually seen within one to two weeks.

Your first appointment

What to expect at your first visit

Whether you come to the office or join by video, the first appointment is a full psychiatric evaluation, about an hour: your story, sleep, mood, focus, health history, and goals, explored in an unhurried conversation. Bring your medication list and insurance card; prior records help but are not required.

You leave with an initial diagnosis explained plainly and a plan you helped shape. Prescriptions are sent electronically to your pharmacy, whether that is at Fair Oaks Mall, Fairfax Towne Center, or anywhere else, and your first follow-up is set for two to four weeks out.

Medication management

Medication management, ten minutes away

Alice manages the complete range of psychiatric medications: antidepressants, anti-anxiety medications, ADHD stimulants and non-stimulants, mood stabilizers, and sleep medications. Because the office is genuinely nearby, Fair Oaks patients can mix in-person medication reviews with video follow-ups however life dictates.

Expect plain-language explanations, deliberate dose adjustments based on how you actually respond, and a standing invitation to report side effects early rather than toughing them out until the next visit.

Supportive psychotherapy

Supportive psychotherapy in every appointment

Appointments include supportive psychotherapy: practical work on the stress, thought loops, and habits underneath the symptoms. For many patients, this integrated care, medication and meaningful conversation with one clinician, is the right fit without adding a separate weekly commitment.

When structured therapy would add value, Alice refers to well-matched therapists nearby, including Vietnamese-speaking options, and coordinates care with them directly.

Quick answers

Questions from Fair Oaks patients

How close is the office to Fair Oaks, really?

About ten minutes: 3060 Williams Drive, Suite 300, in Merrifield, reached easily from Route 50 or I-66, with free on-site parking.

Can I alternate between in-person and video visits?

Yes, freely. Many Fair Oaks patients do their evaluation in person and follow-ups by video, or the reverse. The care is identical either way.

Do you treat hospital employees confidentially?

Completely. Records stay within this independent practice, HIPAA-protected, and never touch your employer’s systems.

Is ADHD evaluation available in person?

Yes. The careful two to three visit evaluation can be done fully in person, fully by video, or mixed, whichever you prefer.

What insurance do you accept?

Most major plans: Aetna, Anthem, Cigna, Medicare, and Medicaid among them. Coverage is verified before your first visit, and self-pay rates are posted openly.

How fast can I get in?

New patients are typically seen within one to two weeks, in person or by video.

Do you conduct visits in Vietnamese?

Yes, entirely: evaluations, medication discussions, and follow-ups in Tiếng Việt, no interpreter needed. Family members are welcome when helpful.

Is there parking, and is the building accessible?

Free on-site parking directly at the building, with accessible entry to the suite. No parking garages, no validation tickets.

Psychiatrist or PMHNP?

Looking for a psychiatrist in Fair Oaks?

Many people searching for a psychiatrist in Fair Oaks end up here, so it is worth being clear about who you would be seeing. Alice Tran is a board certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP-BC), not a psychiatrist. For outpatient care in Virginia that distinction matters less than most people expect: she evaluates and diagnoses mental health conditions, prescribes and manages psychiatric medication including ADHD medication, and provides supportive therapy.

The practical differences tend to favor the patient. Psychiatrist offices in this area commonly quote waits of one to three months and many do not take insurance. This practice usually sees new patients within one to two weeks, accepts most major plans including Medicare and Medicaid, and gives every new patient a full 60 minute first appointment. Alice also trained under psychiatrist Dr. Errol Segall, MD, who brings over 50 years of clinical experience.
