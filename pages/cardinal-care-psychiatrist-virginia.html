Cardinal Care

Cardinal Care is the program.
Your health plan is what actually matters.

This is the most common insurance question we get, and the answer has a wrinkle worth understanding before you book.

The confusing part

Cardinal Care is not an insurance company

Cardinal Care is the name Virginia gives to its entire Medicaid program. It is not a carrier, and there is no such thing as a Cardinal Care card that works everywhere on its own.

Under Cardinal Care Managed Care, your actual coverage runs through one of five health plans. That plan is who processes your claims and who decides which providers are in network for you. So when a benefits check comes back saying simply “Cardinal Care,” that does not yet answer the question. The next step is finding out which of the five plans you are enrolled in.

This matters for a practical reason: being in network with one Cardinal Care plan does not automatically mean being in network with the other four. Credentialing happens plan by plan.

  • ✓ Cardinal Care is the Virginia Medicaid program itself
  • ✓ Your coverage is administered by one of five managed care plans
  • ✓ The plan name is printed on your member card, not just “Cardinal Care”
  • ✓ Network status is decided by that plan, not by the Cardinal Care program

Find your plan

How to tell which one you have

It takes about two minutes, and it saves a wasted appointment.

01

Look at your member card

The plan name is on the front. You are looking for Anthem HealthKeepers Plus, Humana Healthy Horizons, Aetna Better Health, UnitedHealthcare Community Plan, or Sentara Health Plans.

02

Or check your Cardinal Care account

You can see your assigned health plan by logging in to the Virginia Medicaid member portal, or by calling Virginia Medicaid Managed Care Helpline.

03

Call us with the plan name

Once you know which plan you have, call (703) 791-9099 and we will confirm your current network status before scheduling anything.

04

If your plan is not in network yet

Tell us anyway. Several plans are in credentialing now, and we can let you know when your plan goes live rather than leaving you to guess.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take Cardinal Care?

Partly, and the honest answer depends on your specific plan. Cardinal Care is the Virginia Medicaid program rather than one insurance company, and your coverage runs through one of five health plans. This practice is currently in network with Anthem HealthKeepers Plus. Several other plans are in credentialing now, and Sentara Health Plans is not in network because that panel is closed.

Why can you take one Cardinal Care plan and not another?

Each of the five health plans maintains its own provider network and its own credentialing process. A practice has to be approved separately by each one, and plans sometimes close their panels when they have enough providers in an area. It is frustrating and it is outside a provider’s control.

I have Anthem HealthKeepers Plus. Can I book now?

Yes. That plan is in network today, so you can schedule and your visits are billed in network.

I have Sentara. What are my options?

Sentara Health Plans is not in network here and their panel is currently closed. You can ask Virginia Medicaid about switching plans during an open enrollment period, or ask us for other options. We would rather point you somewhere useful than have you book a visit that will not be covered.

My plan is in credentialing. Should I wait?

Call and tell us which plan you have. We can note it and let you know when the contract goes live. If you need care sooner than that, we can talk about self-pay or help you look at alternatives, but nobody should sit untreated waiting on paperwork.

Do I need a referral?

No. Cardinal Care plans do not require a referral for outpatient behavioral health.

Can visits be conducted in Vietnamese?

Yes. Evaluation, medication management, and supportive therapy are all available fully in Vietnamese, with no interpreter involved.

A note on urgency

If you are in crisis right now, please do not wait for an appointment.
