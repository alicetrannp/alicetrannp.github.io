How it works

Online psychiatry, done the careful way.

Telepsychiatry has grown up. For evaluations, medication management, and follow-up of depression, anxiety, ADHD, and related conditions, decades of research now show outcomes equivalent to in-person care, with better appointment attendance because the logistics finally fit real life. What separates good online psychiatry from a prescription mill is not the video screen; it is whether the clinician on the other side takes the time to get the diagnosis right.

This practice is one clinician, not an app. You see the same provider every visit: Alice Tran, dual board-certified (PMHNP-BC, FNP-BC) and trained under a psychiatrist with more than fifty years of experience. Evaluations run a full hour, ADHD diagnoses take the proper two to three visits, and medication decisions are explained in plain language, in English or Tiếng Việt.

The mechanics are simple: book online, complete intake forms from your phone, and join through a secure link, no downloads. Prescriptions, including ADHD medications when clinically appropriate, go electronically to your local Virginia pharmacy the same day. Lab work, when needed, is ordered to a location near you. And unlike national telehealth apps, there is a real office in Fairfax you can visit whenever an in-person appointment feels right.

Quick answers

Online psychiatry questions from Virginia patients

Is an online psychiatric evaluation as thorough as in person?

Yes. The psychiatric interview is conversation and observation, and both transfer fully to video. Evaluations here run a full hour regardless of format, and research shows equivalent outcomes for this scope of care.

Can ADHD medication be prescribed online in Virginia?

When clinically appropriate, yes, after a proper two to three visit evaluation, with prescriptions sent to your local pharmacy and the same monitoring as in-person care.

What do I need for an appointment?

A phone, tablet, or computer with a camera, a private spot anywhere in Virginia, and a decent connection. If video drops, you can rejoin on the same link rather than being rescheduled, and the office will call to help you reconnect so the visit can be completed on video.

Does insurance cover online visits?

Yes. Virginia parity rules mean telehealth visits are covered like office visits, and this practice bills them identically. Most major plans accepted, including Aetna, Anthem, Cigna, Medicare, and Medicaid.
