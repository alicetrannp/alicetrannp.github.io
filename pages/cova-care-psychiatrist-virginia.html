Commonwealth of Virginia

Psychiatric care for Virginia state employees.
In network with the Commonwealth of Virginia PPO.

If you work for a Virginia state agency or public university and carry the Commonwealth of Virginia health plan, you are already in network here.

Who this is for

You work for the Commonwealth, and your plan is Anthem underneath

The Commonwealth of Virginia health plan covers employees of state agencies and Virginia public colleges and universities, along with their families. The medical network is administered by Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, which is why your card carries the Anthem logo even though the plan is the Commonwealth’s.

Alice Tran Psychiatric Care is contracted with the Commonwealth of Virginia PPO. That means your visits are billed in network, at in-network rates, with no referral required.

  • ✓ State agency employees across Virginia
  • ✓ Faculty and staff at Virginia public universities, including George Mason, which sits minutes from our Fairfax office
  • ✓ Covered spouses and adult dependents on the same plan
  • ✓ Retirees enrolled in a Commonwealth plan through Anthem
What is covered

What your plan covers here

Behavioral health is a covered medical benefit under the Commonwealth plan. In practice, that means the visits below are billed to Anthem the same way any other specialist visit would be.

  • ✓ Comprehensive psychiatric evaluation, 60 minutes, for a full diagnostic picture
  • ✓ Ongoing medication management, 30 or 45 minutes
  • ✓ Supportive therapy woven into medication visits
  • ✓ ADHD evaluation and treatment for adults
  • ✓ Telehealth video visits from anywhere in Virginia, billed the same as an office visit
  • ✓ In-person visits at our Fairfax office, with free parking
  • ✓ Full visits in Vietnamese at no extra cost and with no interpreter needed
Before your first visit

How to confirm your benefits in five minutes

You do not need a referral, but it is always worth confirming your own numbers before you book. Here is exactly what to ask.

01

Call the number on the back of your card

Ask for behavioral health or mental health benefits. The Commonwealth plan has its own benefit structure, so the general medical line may route you elsewhere.

02

Ask three specific questions

What is my copay for an outpatient behavioral health visit, have I met my deductible, and is telehealth covered at the same rate as an office visit?

03

Confirm the provider type

Ask whether a psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner, or PMHNP, is covered the same as a psychiatrist. On the Commonwealth plan the answer is yes, but it is good to hear it from them.

04

Book online

Once you know your copay, you can book directly. We verify benefits again on our end before your first visit, so nothing surprises you later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Commonwealth of Virginia plan the same as regular Anthem?

Not exactly. The Commonwealth of Virginia PPO is the state’s own health plan, with Anthem administering the provider network and claims. We hold contracts for both, so either way you are in network with us.

Do I need a referral from my primary care doctor?

No. The Commonwealth PPO does not require a referral for outpatient behavioral health. You can book directly.

I work at George Mason. Does that count?

Yes. Faculty and staff at Virginia public colleges and universities are covered under the Commonwealth plan, and our Fairfax office is a short drive from the Mason campus.

Can I be seen by video instead of coming in?

Yes. All psychiatric visits are available by secure video anywhere in Virginia, and psychiatric visits at this practice are conducted by video rather than by telephone. Many state employees find a video visit easier to fit around a work schedule.

Do you see spouses and children on my plan?

We see adults, including covered spouses and adult dependents. We do not currently treat young children.

What if I have the Commonwealth high deductible plan?

You are still in network, but you will pay the contracted rate toward your deductible until it is met, rather than a flat copay. Call the number on your card to check where you stand for the year.

A note on urgency

If you are in crisis right now, please do not wait for an appointment.

Call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Free, confidential, available 24/7.
For emergencies, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.

Alice Tran Psychiatric Care does not operate 24/7 and does not provide crisis services. Emails, voicemails, text or portal messages are typically responded to within 24 to 72 business hours.
