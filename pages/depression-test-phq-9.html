Free Self-Assessment

Depression Test
the PHQ-9 screener

The PHQ-9 is the nine-question depression screener used by clinicians everywhere. It takes two minutes, your answers never leave this page, and the result comes with an honest explanation of what it means.

Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by any of the following problems? Your answers are scored right here in your browser. Nothing is saved, stored, or sent anywhere. This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis, and it does not replace an evaluation with a licensed clinician.

1. Little interest or pleasure in doing things

2. Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless

3. Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much

4. Feeling tired or having little energy

5. Poor appetite or overeating

6. Feeling bad about yourself, or that you are a failure, or have let yourself or your family down

7. Trouble concentrating on things, such as reading the newspaper or watching television

8. Moving or speaking so slowly that other people could have noticed? Or the opposite, being so fidgety or restless that you have been moving around a lot more than usual

9. Thoughts that you would be better off dead or of hurting yourself in some way

Your answer to the last question matters most.

If you are having thoughts of being better off dead or of hurting yourself, please reach out now: call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, 24/7), text HOME to 741741, or go to your nearest emergency room. These thoughts are a symptom that deserves immediate support, and help works.

A score of 10 or higher is the standard threshold where clinicians recommend a full evaluation. The PHQ-9 is a screening tool, not a diagnosis; grief, thyroid problems, sleep disorders, and bipolar depression all need a clinician to tell apart.

Keep your score: take a screenshot or jot the number down. Bringing it to your first appointment gives the evaluation a running start. Nothing on this page is saved, so your result exists only on your screen.

Ready to talk through your result?

Alice Tran, PMHNP-BC provides full evaluations in person in Fairfax and via telehealth across Virginia, in English and Vietnamese. New patients are typically seen within one to two weeks. No referral needed.
