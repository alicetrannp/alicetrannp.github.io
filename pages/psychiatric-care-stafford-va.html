About this practice

Serious care for a county the system overlooks.

Stafford County has grown faster than nearly any community its size in Virginia, but psychiatric care has not grown with it. Residents routinely face months-long waits or referrals up to Fredericksburg, Woodbridge, or even Northern Virginia proper. For a county full of Marines and their families, federal commuters, teachers, and first responders, that gap has real costs.

The Stafford patterns Alice sees are distinct: military and veteran families around Quantico managing deployments, reintegration, and the particular stress of always being ready to move; PTSD that never got addressed because seeking help felt like a career risk; commuters spending ten or more hours a week on I-95 and arriving home with nothing left; and parents in Embrey Mill and North Stafford holding households together on schedules that would break anyone.

Alice Tran is a dual board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner (PMHNP-BC, FNP-BC) treating adults ages 18 to 65 for anxiety, depression, ADHD, OCD, PTSD, bipolar disorder, insomnia, and burnout. Trained under a psychiatrist with more than fifty years of experience, she provides psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and supportive psychotherapy, in English and Vietnamese.

Everything happens by secure telehealth for Stafford patients: evaluations, follow-ups, and prescriptions sent to your local pharmacy, whether you are in Aquia Harbour, Falmouth, or off Garrisonville Road. Most major insurance plans are accepted, including Medicare and Medicaid, no referral is needed, and new patients are typically seen within one to two weeks.

Your first appointment

How the first appointment works

The first visit is a comprehensive hour-long psychiatric evaluation over secure video. It covers what brought you in, sleep, mood, focus, energy, medical background, service history when relevant, and what you have tried before. It is conversational and judgment-free; you set the depth on anything difficult.

You leave with a working diagnosis in plain language and a treatment plan built with you. Prescriptions go to your Stafford pharmacy the same day when medication is part of the plan, and a follow-up two to four weeks later makes sure the plan gets adjusted on evidence rather than left to drift.

Medication management

Medication management you can trust

Alice prescribes and manages the full spectrum: antidepressants, anti-anxiety medications, stimulant and non-stimulant ADHD medications, mood stabilizers, sleep medications, and targeted options like prazosin for trauma nightmares. Choices are made with you, weighing symptoms, medical history, side-effect priorities, and cost.

Follow-up is where results happen: closer visits during adjustments, quick answers to refill questions, and honest reassessment when something is only half-working. Nobody gets parked on an ineffective dose because nobody asked.

Supportive psychotherapy

Supportive psychotherapy included

Every appointment carries room for real conversation: the thinking patterns anxiety runs on, the numbness that follows years of pushing through, the strain deployments put on both the deployed and the ones holding down the home front. This supportive psychotherapy is integrated with medication care rather than split across providers.

When structured trauma therapy such as EMDR or prolonged exposure is the right next step, Alice refers to specialists and coordinates, so therapy and medication reinforce each other instead of running on parallel tracks.

Quick answers

Questions from Stafford patients

Is everything really done by telehealth?

Yes, for Stafford patients: evaluation, medication management, and follow-up, all by secure video, with labs ordered near you when needed. The Fairfax office exists for anyone who ever wants an in-person visit.

Do you work with military families and TRICARE?

Alice regularly treats military-connected adults; contact the office to verify current participation for your specific plan. Care is fully independent of any command and protected by HIPAA.

Will seeking care hurt a military career or clearance?

Untreated symptoms are consistently a bigger risk than treated ones, and current federal guidance reflects that. Concerns about documentation are fair game at the first visit; you will get straight answers, not brush-offs.

Can PTSD really be treated over video?

Evaluation and medication management, yes, and many trauma patients strongly prefer their own safe space. Where intensive trauma therapy is needed, you get a coordinated referral.

How soon can I be seen?

Typically within one to two weeks, compared to the months-long waits common south of the Occoquan.

Do you take Medicaid and Medicare?

Yes, along with most major commercial plans including Aetna, Anthem, and Cigna. Self-pay rates are posted transparently.

Can I keep my appointment if I get stationed elsewhere?

Your care can continue while you are located in Virginia. If a PCS takes you out of state, Alice helps transition your care properly rather than dropping the thread.

Do you treat both spouses or family members?

Yes, adults 18 to 65, each with their own confidential care. Many Stafford households have more than one person quietly carrying too much.

Psychiatrist or PMHNP?

Looking for a psychiatrist in Stafford?

Many people searching for a psychiatrist in Stafford end up here, so it is worth being clear about who you would be seeing. Alice Tran is a board certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP-BC), not a psychiatrist. For outpatient care in Virginia that distinction matters less than most people expect: she evaluates and diagnoses mental health conditions, prescribes and manages psychiatric medication including ADHD medication, and provides supportive therapy.

The practical differences tend to favor the patient. Psychiatrist offices in this area commonly quote waits of one to three months and many do not take insurance. This practice usually sees new patients within one to two weeks, accepts most major plans including Medicare and Medicaid, and gives every new patient a full 60 minute first appointment. Alice also trained under psychiatrist Dr. Errol Segall, MD, who brings over 50 years of clinical experience.
