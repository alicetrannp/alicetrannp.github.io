LGBTQ+ and transgender support resources

Affirming places to talk, find community, and get care. Many of these lines are answered by LGBTQ+ and trans peers, and you can call on an ordinary hard day.

Prepared by Alice Tran, PMHNP-BC, FNP-BC · Alice Tran Psychiatric Care · Updated November 2026

All patient resources

How to use this sheet

  • These lines are answered by people from the LGBTQ+ community. You do not need to be in crisis, and you do not need to be sure of your identity, to call.
  • Hours are listed in Eastern time and can change. Check the website before you rely on a line.

Transgender and nonbinary peer support

  • Trans Lifeline: (877) 565-8860. Monday to Friday 1 pm to 9 pm. translifeline.org
  • Every call is answered by a trans or nonbinary peer.
  • Calls are anonymous and confidential. There are no intake questions.
  • Their published policy is that they do not contact emergency services without your explicit request.
  • Press 2 for Spanish. If the line is busy, try again a little later.

LGBTQ+ lines for any age

  • LGBT National Hotline: (888) 843-4564. Monday to Friday 2 pm to 11 pm, Saturday noon to 5 pm. Confidential peer support on sexual orientation, gender identity, coming out, relationships, and more.
  • LGBT National Coming Out Support Hotline: (888) 688-5428. For anyone thinking about coming out, at any age.
  • The SQSH: (844) 785-7774. Friday to Monday 2 pm to 8 pm. Identity-affirming emotional support and resource referrals.
  • Hours and online chat for the LGBT National Help Center lines: lgbthotline.org

Young adults and older adults

  • The Trevor Project, for ages 24 and under: (866) 488-7386, or text START to 678-678. Open any time. Trained crisis counselors.
  • LGBT National Youth Talkline, for ages 25 and under: (800) 246-7743.
  • LGBT National Senior Hotline, for ages 50 and over: (888) 234-7243.
  • SAGE LGBTQ+ Elder Hotline: (877) 360-5428. Open any time, in English and Spanish.

If you are in immediate danger

  • 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, any time, day or night.
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741, any time.
  • 911 or the nearest emergency room for a medical emergency or immediate danger.
  • The peer lines on this sheet are for support and connection. They are not emergency services, and most are not open around the clock.

Affirming medical care near Northern Virginia

  • Inova Pride Clinic, Falls Church: primary care for LGBTQ+ patients, including gender-affirming hormone care and HIV prevention. inova.org/prideclinic
  • Whitman-Walker Health, Washington, DC: medical care, behavioral health, and legal services with long experience in LGBTQ+ and HIV care. whitman-walker.org
  • OutCare Health: a national directory of affirming providers. outcarehealth.org

Community and family

  • PFLAG: support groups for LGBTQ+ people, parents, partners, and families. Find a local chapter at pflag.org
  • Equality Virginia: statewide advocacy and a resource list for LGBTQ+ Virginians. equalityvirginia.org
  • LGBT Near Me: a searchable database of local groups, centers, and services. lgbtnearme.org
  • Peer groups online: see the peer support lines and community resources handout.

Names, ID documents, and legal questions

  • Advocates for Trans Equality: state-by-state guidance on updating a name or gender marker on identity documents. transequality.org
  • Lambda Legal Help Desk: information on discrimination related to sexual orientation, gender identity, or HIV status. lambdalegal.org

Your care with us

  • Tell us the name and pronouns you use. We will use them in your visits and in our messages to you.
  • If your legal name differs from the name you use, let us know which name is safe to use on voicemail, mail, and text messages.
  • What you share about your identity is part of your confidential health record.
  • You decide what you want to talk about. We will not assume your identity is the reason you are here.

Good to know

  • Peer supporters are not clinicians. They do not diagnose or give medication advice.
  • These organizations are independent of this practice. A listing here is not an endorsement of everything on their websites.
  • Several listings were drawn from the resource list published by THRIVE Lifeline. Details were checked in November 2026.
