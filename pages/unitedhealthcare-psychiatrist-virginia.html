Looking for a Psychiatric Provider in Virginia Who Takes UnitedHealthcare?

Alice Tran Psychiatric Care is in network with UnitedHealthcare and its associated plans, including Optum and United Behavioral Health, for adults across Virginia. In person in Fairfax or by secure video, in English and Vietnamese.

Using your plan

How UnitedHealthcare covers psychiatric care

UnitedHealthcare covers outpatient mental health care, including psychiatric evaluations and medication management visits with a board-certified psychiatric nurse practitioner. For most members the behavioral health side of the plan is administered by Optum, which is why your card or portal may show Optum rather than UnitedHealthcare when you look up mental health benefits.

What you pay depends on your specific plan: a copay, coinsurance, or the remainder of a deductible. Before your first appointment, call the member services number on the back of your card to confirm your outpatient behavioral health benefit, ask whether a referral or prior authorization is needed, and verify your cost share. This practice will also verify your eligibility and benefits before your visit.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does Alice Tran accept UnitedHealthcare?

    Yes. Alice Tran is in network with UnitedHealthcare and its associated commercial and behavioral health plans, including Optum, United Behavioral Health, MAMSI and MD-IPA plans, UnitedHealthcare Community Plan for Cardinal Care, the Virginia Exchange, and Oscar Health Plan. UnitedHealthcare Medicare is still processing.

    Is UnitedHealthcare Medicare accepted?

    Not yet. UnitedHealthcare Medicare and Medicare Advantage credentialing is still being processed as of August 2026. Traditional Medicare is accepted. Please call (703) 791-9099 to check the current status before booking.

    Why does my UnitedHealthcare card say Optum for mental health?

    UnitedHealthcare administers behavioral health benefits through Optum for most plans, so mental health visits are processed under Optum or United Behavioral Health. Both are in network with this practice, so either one works.

    Do I need a referral to use UnitedHealthcare for psychiatric care?

    In most cases, no. Most UnitedHealthcare plans allow you to see a behavioral health provider without a referral, though some plan types require one. Call the member services number on your card to confirm what your specific plan requires.

    Does this practice accept UnitedHealthcare workers’ compensation?

    No. This practice does not provide workers’ compensation care or workers’ compensation evaluations of any kind.

    How soon can I be seen with UnitedHealthcare?

    New patients are typically seen within one to two weeks, with a full 60-minute initial evaluation. Visits are available in person in Fairfax or by secure video anywhere in Virginia.

    A note on urgency

    If you are in crisis right now, please do not wait for an appointment.

    Call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Free, confidential, available 24/7.
    For emergencies, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.

    Alice Tran Psychiatric Care does not operate 24/7 and does not provide crisis services. Emails, voicemails, text or portal messages are typically responded to within 24 to 72 business hours.
