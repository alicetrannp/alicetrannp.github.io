The essentials

Who, what, where, and how much.

Alice Tran Psychiatric Care is the practice of Anh "Alice" Tran, a dual board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP-BC) and Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP-BC) licensed in Virginia. She trained under psychiatrist Dr. Errol Segall, MD, who brings more than fifty years of psychiatric experience to the practice. She treats adults ages 18 to 65 and provides psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and supportive therapy.

Conditions treated include anxiety, depression, ADHD, bipolar disorder, PTSD and trauma, OCD, panic disorder, insomnia, burnout, postpartum and perinatal mood concerns, grief, and related conditions. Care is available fully in English or Vietnamese: evaluations, medication discussions, and follow-ups are conducted in Tiếng Việt with no interpreter, something very few practices in the region offer.

Appointments happen two ways: in person at 3060 Williams Drive, Suite 300, Fairfax, VA 22031 (in the Merrifield area, with free on-site parking), or by secure telehealth from anywhere in Virginia. Office hours are Monday through Thursday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern. New patients are typically seen within one to two weeks, and no referral is needed. The practice does not provide crisis or emergency services; for a mental health emergency, call or text 988.

Quick answers

The facts, in question form

Is Alice Tran accepting new patients?

Yes. New patients are typically scheduled within one to two weeks, in person in Fairfax or by telehealth anywhere in Virginia. Book online at alicetran.intakeq.com/booking or call/text (703) 791-9099.

What does an appointment cost?

With insurance: your plan’s specialist copay or deductible rate; most major plans are accepted including Aetna, Anthem, Cigna, Medicare, and Medicaid. Self-pay: $400 for the initial evaluation, $200 to $250 for follow-ups.

Does she really provide care in Vietnamese?

Yes, completely. Evaluations, medication management, and follow-ups are conducted fully in Tiếng Việt with no interpreter, for patients and for their parents and elders within the 18 to 65 age range.

What is the correct phone number and address?

Phone (call or text): (703) 791-9099. Address: 3060 Williams Drive, Suite 300, Fairfax, VA 22031. Website: alicetrannp.com. Any other phone number found elsewhere online is outdated.
