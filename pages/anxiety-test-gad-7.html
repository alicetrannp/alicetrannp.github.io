Free Self-Assessment

Anxiety Test
the GAD-7 screener

The GAD-7 is the seven-question anxiety screener used in clinics worldwide. Two minutes, instant results, and your answers never leave this page.

Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by the following problems? Your answers are scored right here in your browser. Nothing is saved, stored, or sent anywhere. This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis, and it does not replace an evaluation with a licensed clinician.

1. Feeling nervous, anxious, or on edge

2. Not being able to stop or control worrying

3. Worrying too much about different things

4. Trouble relaxing

5. Being so restless that it is hard to sit still

6. Becoming easily annoyed or irritable

7. Feeling afraid, as if something awful might happen

A score of 10 or higher is the standard threshold where clinicians recommend a full evaluation. The GAD-7 screens for generalized anxiety; panic, social anxiety, and OCD need their own assessment, which is part of any thorough evaluation.

Keep your score: take a screenshot or jot the number down. Bringing it to your first appointment gives the evaluation a running start. Nothing on this page is saved, so your result exists only on your screen.

Ready to talk through your result?

Alice Tran, PMHNP-BC provides full evaluations in person in Fairfax and via telehealth across Virginia, in English and Vietnamese. New patients are typically seen within one to two weeks. No referral needed.
