Looking for a Psychiatric Provider in Virginia Who Takes Humana?

Alice Tran Psychiatric Care is in network with Humana Medicare Advantage plans for adults across Virginia. In person in Fairfax or by secure video, in English and Vietnamese.

Using your plan

How Humana covers psychiatric care

Humana's health plans in Virginia are Medicare Advantage plans: PPO, Regional PPO, HMO, HMO-POS, Private Fee-for-Service, and Special Needs Plans for people who have both Medicare and Medicaid, who live with diabetes or heart disease, or who live in a care facility. All of them cover outpatient mental health care, including psychiatric evaluations and medication management visits with a board-certified psychiatric nurse practitioner.

What you pay is usually a fixed copay for a specialist or mental health visit, and it differs from plan to plan. HMO plans may ask you to choose a primary care provider and may require a referral for specialists; PPO plans generally do not. Before your first appointment, call the member services number on the back of your card, confirm your outpatient mental health benefit, and ask whether a referral is needed. This practice also verifies your eligibility before your visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Alice Tran accept Humana?

Yes. Alice Tran Psychiatric Care is in network with Humana Medicare Advantage plans in Virginia as of October 2026, including HumanaChoice PPO, Humana Gold Plus HMO, Humana USAA Honor, Humana Gold Choice PFFS, and Humana Special Needs Plans.

Is a Humana card always a Medicare plan?

In Virginia, yes. Humana no longer sells employer or individual commercial medical plans, so a Humana health card today is a Medicare Advantage plan, a Medicare Special Needs Plan, or a group Medicare plan through a former employer. If your card says Humana Military, that is TRICARE, which is handled separately.

Do I need a referral to see a psychiatric provider with Humana?

It depends on your plan type. Humana PPO and PFFS plans generally let you see a specialist without a referral. Humana HMO plans may require one from your primary care provider. Call the number on your card to confirm.

I have both Medicare and Medicaid. Am I covered?

Yes. Humana Dual Select and Humana Gold Plus SNP-DE are Dual Special Needs Plans for people with both Medicare and Medicaid, and both are in network. Your cost share on these plans is usually very low.

Is Humana Military the same as Humana?

No. Humana Military administers TRICARE for the military community and is a separate contract from Humana Medicare Advantage. This practice is set up for both, but the coverage rules differ. See the TRICARE page for Humana Military details.

How soon can I be seen with Humana?

New patients are typically seen within one to two weeks, with a full 60-minute initial evaluation. Visits are available in person in Fairfax or by secure video anywhere in Virginia.

If you are in crisis right now, please do not wait for an appointment.
