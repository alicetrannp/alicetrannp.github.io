Blue Cross Blue Shield

Yes, we take Blue Cross Blue Shield.
Here is how to tell which plan you have.

Blue Cross Blue Shield is not one company, which is why this question is more confusing than it should be. This page sorts out which card you are holding and what it means here.

Why this is confusing

One name, many companies

Blue Cross Blue Shield is an association of independent companies, each serving its own region, all using the same name and the same familiar shield. There is no single national Blue Cross.

In Virginia, the Blue Cross company is Anthem. In Maryland and Washington DC it is CareFirst. Other states have their own. On top of that sit programs that use the Blue Cross network without being a state company at all, like the Federal Employee Program for federal workers and BlueCard for members traveling or living outside their home state.

The practical upshot is that "do you take Blue Cross Blue Shield" has a longer answer than yes or no. For this practice the answer is yes across all the common cases, but which page below applies depends on what is printed on your card.

  • ✓ Your card says Anthem and you live in Virginia
  • ✓ Your card says HealthKeepers or KeyCare, which are Anthem products
  • ✓ Your card says Blue Cross Blue Shield Service Benefit Plan or FEP, the federal employee plan
  • ✓ Your card is Blue Cross from another state, such as CareFirst from Maryland or DC
  • ✓ Your plan came through Virginia state or local government employment

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept Blue Cross Blue Shield?

Yes. Alice Tran Psychiatric Care is in network with Blue Cross Blue Shield in Virginia, including Anthem, HealthKeepers, KeyCare, the Federal Employee Program, the Commonwealth of Virginia plan, The Local Choice, and out-of-state plans through BlueCard.

Is Anthem the same as Blue Cross Blue Shield?

Anthem is the Blue Cross Blue Shield company for Virginia. Blue Cross Blue Shield is an association of independent regional companies rather than a single insurer, so the name on your card depends on which state issued your plan.

My card says CareFirst. Can I still be seen?

Usually yes, if your plan is a PPO. CareFirst is the Blue Cross company for Maryland and Washington DC, and the BlueCard program lets those benefits travel with you into Virginia. Call the number on your card and ask about out-of-state outpatient behavioral health.

Do I need a referral?

On most Blue Cross plans, no referral is required for outpatient behavioral health. HMO products and some federal options have their own rules, so it is worth a quick call to confirm.

How do I know which Blue Cross plan I have?

Look at the front of your card. The company name appears at the top, the plan name is usually beneath it, and a suitcase symbol indicates BlueCard eligibility. If it is still unclear, call the number on the back and ask them to name your plan.

What will a visit cost me?

That depends on your specific plan and where you are in your deductible, which is why no figure is published here. Call the number on your card, ask specifically about outpatient behavioral health, and you will have an exact answer in a few minutes.

A note on urgency

If you are in crisis right now, please do not wait for an appointment.
