The Daily Reset

A step-by-step check-in for hard days. Answer one simple question at a time, and do one small thing before the next.

In crisis? Call or text 988. Emergency: 911.

How it works

  • One question at a time. No lists to plan, and no wrong answers.
  • Each answer leads to one small action, or moves you on.
  • Start with the body: food, water, medication, sleep, movement. Mood is easier to judge after that.
  • Choose a different path for panic, a hard day, sadness, or a low mood that has lasted two weeks or more.
  • Grounding and slow breathing are built in. Use them any time.
  • Your answers stay on your device. Nothing is saved or sent.

When to use it

  • When you feel off and cannot say why
  • When you cannot decide what to do first
  • First thing in the morning, as a routine
  • Between visits, alongside your treatment plan
