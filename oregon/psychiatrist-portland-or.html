About this practice

Care that fits how Portland actually lives.

Portland has no shortage of therapists and a serious shortage of prescribers. People often spend months in therapy that is helping, hit the point where medication is worth discussing, and then run straight into a six-month waitlist. That gap is where this practice is useful.

Visits are by secure video, which in Portland means no crossing the river at rush hour, no parking, and no explaining a two-hour absence. The initial evaluation runs a full 60 minutes. Follow-ups are 30 or 45 minutes, long enough to actually review how a medication is landing rather than just refill it.

Portland care is self-pay only, so there is no plan to verify, no prior authorization, and no diagnosis submitted to an insurer. For people in tech, healthcare, food service, freelance work, and the many Portlanders on high-deductible plans, the published rate is often clearer and sometimes cheaper than working through a deductible.

Adult ADHD, evaluated properly

Not a ten-minute checklist. A careful evaluation over two to three visits that considers sleep, anxiety, and the rest of the picture before landing on an answer.

Anxiety and depression in working adults

Functioning well on paper and running empty underneath is the most common presentation here, and it responds to treatment.

When therapy alone is not enough

Good therapy plus the right medication often works better than either alone. Coordination with your existing therapist is welcome.

Vietnamese-speaking care

Portland has one of the West Coast’s established Vietnamese communities. Care in Vietnamese is available directly, with no interpreter in the room.

Quick answers

What Portland patients want to know

How soon can I actually be seen?

Usually within one to two weeks. There is no waitlist and no referral requirement, so booking is the whole process.

Are visits in person anywhere in Portland?

No. All Oregon care is by secure video. There is no Portland office. The advantage is that a visit fits in a lunch break from anywhere in the metro, from Beaverton to Gresham.

What does it cost in Portland?

The initial 60-minute evaluation is $400. Follow-ups are $200 for 30 minutes or $250 for 45. Oregon care is self-pay only, so that published rate is the amount you pay.

Can you prescribe ADHD medication to a Portland patient?

ADHD is evaluated and treated here, and stimulant medication is prescribed when it is clinically appropriate, with regular monitoring and prescription-monitoring database checks. What is possible in a given case is discussed openly at the evaluation.

I already have a therapist in Portland. Can you just handle medication?

Yes, and that is a common arrangement. Keep your therapist, add a prescriber. With your permission the two sides coordinate so you are not carrying messages between them.

Do you see patients outside Portland proper?

Yes, anywhere in Oregon. Beaverton, Hillsboro, Gresham, Lake Oswego, Vancouver-adjacent Oregon suburbs, and the rest of the state are all served by the same secure video visit.
