About this practice

Why Oregon care is self-pay, and what that buys you.

This practice is not paneled with Oregon health plans, so Oregon visits are paid directly. That is a real cost, and it is stated plainly rather than buried. What it removes is also real: no prior authorization before a medication can be tried, no visit length dictated by a billing code, no claim, and no psychiatric diagnosis reported to a health plan that may follow you into future underwriting decisions.

It also removes the waiting. Much of the delay in Oregon psychiatry is not clinical, it is administrative. Skipping that machinery is a large part of why new patients here are usually seen in one to two weeks rather than six months.

Rates are flat and published. There is no facility fee, no separate charge for the prescription itself, and no bill that arrives weeks later with a number you did not expect.

Initial evaluation  ·  $400

Up to 60 minutes by secure video. Your history, current concerns, and life context, ending with a plan you understand and agreed to.

Follow-up  ·  $200 / 30 min

Medication management and supportive therapy. Early on visits may be every two weeks, then they space out as things stabilize.

Extended follow-up  ·  $250 / 45 min

For visits that need more room, whether that is a complicated medication change or a harder stretch of life.

Quick answers

Questions about paying out of pocket

Can I submit these visits to my health plan myself?

This practice does not file claims for Oregon patients and does not participate with Oregon health plans. Whether your plan reimburses anything for an out-of-plan provider is a question for your plan, and the answer is often no. Please do not book on the assumption that you will be reimbursed.

What is a Good Faith Estimate?

Under the federal No Surprises Act, patients who are not using insurance have the right to a written estimate of what their care is expected to cost. You can ask for one before your first visit. If a final bill ends up at least $400 above the estimate, you have the right to dispute it.

Can I use an HSA or FSA card?

Psychiatric visits are generally an eligible medical expense, so HSA and FSA cards are commonly used. Your plan administrator is the final word on what your specific account allows.

When do I pay?

At the time of the visit. A receipt is provided for your records.

What if I miss an appointment?

A missed visit or a cancellation with less than 24 hours notice is charged $100. The reason is simple: that hour was held for you and cannot be given to someone else on short notice.

Is the price different in Portland than elsewhere in Oregon?

No. Every Oregon visit is by secure video and every Oregon patient pays the same published rate, whether you are in Portland, Bend, or a small town on the coast.
