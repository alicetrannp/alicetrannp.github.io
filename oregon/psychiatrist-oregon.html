About this practice

Thoughtful psychiatry, now open to Oregonians.

Oregon has a real shortage of psychiatric prescribers, and the waitlists show it. People wait months for a first appointment, then get twenty minutes and a prescription. This practice is built the other way around: a full 60-minute initial evaluation, follow-ups that are long enough to actually talk, and a provider who explains the reasoning instead of handing down a verdict.

Alice Tran is dual board-certified as a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner and a Family Nurse Practitioner, and trained under a psychiatrist with more than fifty years in practice. Care covers anxiety, depression, ADHD, bipolar disorder, OCD, PTSD, and insomnia in adults.

Oregon visits are self-pay only and always by secure video, so where you live in the state does not change what is available to you. Portland, Eugene, Salem, Bend, or a small town on the coast: the appointment is the same, and so is the price.

Seen in weeks, not months

New patients are typically scheduled within one to two weeks. No referral is needed and there is no waitlist to join.

A full hour to start

The initial evaluation is up to 60 minutes. Complex questions, particularly adult ADHD, may take two to three visits to answer properly.

Transparent self-pay pricing

Oregon care is self-pay only. $400 for the initial evaluation, $200 for a 30-minute follow-up. You know the number before you book.

English and Vietnamese

Care in either language, with no interpreter in the room. Vietnamese-speaking psychiatric prescribers are rare in Oregon.

Quick answers

What Oregon patients ask first

Are you actually licensed in Oregon?

Yes. Alice Tran holds an active Oregon nurse practitioner license and can see adults who are physically located in Oregon at the time of the visit. That is the rule that matters for telehealth: your location during the appointment, not where you are from.

Do you take my insurance in Oregon?

No. Oregon care is self-pay only. Nothing is billed to a health plan, no claim is filed, and no diagnosis is reported to an insurer. You pay a published rate directly and you are told the amount before you book.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book an Oregon appointment directly. Bringing records from a previous prescriber is helpful but never required.

Where in Oregon do you see patients?

Anywhere in the state, by secure video. Portland and the surrounding metro make up most of the Oregon caseload, but Eugene, Salem, Bend, Gresham, Hillsboro, and rural Oregon are all served the same way.

Is there an office I can visit in Oregon?

No. Oregon care is telehealth only. The in-person office is in Fairfax, Virginia. For Oregon patients, every visit is by secure video.

Can you prescribe medication to someone in Oregon?

Yes, within the scope of an Oregon nurse practitioner license. What is appropriate depends on the medication and on your clinical picture, and it is discussed openly at the evaluation rather than promised in advance.

Accepting new patients across Oregon.

Secure video statewide. Self-pay only. English and Vietnamese.
